ABOUT ME


I’m a Unity Gameplay Programmer with 4+ years of experience working on commercial and personal projects. I mainly work on gameplay systems, mechanics, physics, and the technical problems that come with making a game work smoothly.

I enjoy figuring out how things should work, building them in C#, and debugging them when they don’t. I’m particularly interested in physics, game math, system design, AI, and performance. I’m also comfortable working independently or working closely with designers and artists to turn ideas into playable features. Alongside Unity and C#, I’m learning Python and exploring machine learning to see how these technologies can be useful in game development.
